# PRODUCT_BLUEPRINT.md

## Purpose
Define the product intent and user outcomes for the Xerge expo demo.

## Audience
Product owners, operators, engineers, and future AI contributors.

## Responsibilities
- Deliver a 2-4 minute challenge flow that attracts and educates expo visitors.
- Reinforce Xerge's positioning: senior engineering judgment + AI-accelerated delivery.
- Capture consented leads for follow-up.
- Keep operations simple for booth staff.

## Constraints
- Offline-first behavior required.
- No backend dependencies.
- Exactly five startup decision questions.
- Core flow must auto-reset between visitors.

### Expo goal
Generate qualified conversations and follow-up leads while demonstrating CTO-level judgment framing.

### Target users
- Early-stage founders
- Product/engineering leaders at startups
- Investors and startup advisors evaluating execution maturity

### User journey
Idle attract screen → quiz (5 questions) → persona result → Xerge bridge → consented lead capture → thank-you → auto-reset.

### Success metrics
- >70% quiz completion rate
- >30% lead capture from completions
- 2–4 minute completion time
- <10 second reset turnaround

### Core Xerge message
AI can move fast; without senior engineering judgment it can move fast in the wrong direction. Xerge aligns velocity with sound technical direction.
