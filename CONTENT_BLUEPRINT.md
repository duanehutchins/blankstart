# CONTENT_BLUEPRINT.md

## Purpose
Define quiz and messaging content standards for consistent Xerge positioning.

## Audience
Content strategists, product leads, and engineering implementers.

## Responsibilities
- Keep tone practical, founder-friendly, and non-shaming.
- Ensure each question teaches a real startup tradeoff.
- Tie results directly to actionable Xerge value.

## Constraints
- Exactly five questions.
- No trivia or hype copy.
- No guaranteed outcome claims.

### Quiz tone
Sharp, practical, executive-friendly, and educational.

### Question themes
- MVP architecture under uncertainty
- AI-generated code trust vs review
- Build vs buy under runway constraints
- Reliability vs shipping velocity
- Technical debt sequencing

### Persona definitions
- Rebuild Magnet
- Feature Sprinter
- Almost Technical Founder
- Startup Systems Thinker
- AI CTO Material

Each persona includes summary, risk profile, practical next step, and bridge copy.

### Xerge bridge copy
Formula: What your answers suggest → What usually breaks next → How Xerge helps now.
